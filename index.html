Before you start

1Scan a surface

Allow the camera, then move the tablet slowly over a desk or the floor.

2Place the city

When the glowing circle appears, tap it to place the city.

3Explore and tap

Walk around, resize or rotate the city, and tap each smart system.

Smart systems to find

🚦Smart traffic light

A camera sensor sees the bus waiting and turns the light green right on time.

🗑️Smart trash can

Its sensor calls the garbage truck before the bin becomes too full.

☀️Clean power

Blue solar panels and a wind turbine make electricity without smoke.

🌿Plant-covered building

Trees on the balconies of two tall towers cool the buildings and clean the air.

🏠Smart thermostat

The round thermostat learns the temperature people like.

🚌Electric bus

A teal bus with a lightning bolt, like the electric buses between Jbeil and Beirut.

Camera note: this activity uses the camera only inside the browser to understand the surface around the tablet. It does not record or upload video. If markerless AR is unavailable, the activity offers interactive 3D and marker-based backups.
